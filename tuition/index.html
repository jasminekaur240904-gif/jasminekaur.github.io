Sample visual notes: The Tense Time Machine 🚀

One action. Three time zones. Four ways to describe it.

⏪PASTBefore now

I played yesterday.

⏺️PRESENTNow / regularly

I play every day.

⏩FUTUREAfter now

I will play tomorrow.

STEP 1 · Choose WHEN the action happens
⏪Past
➜
⏺️Present
➜
⏩Future
STEP 2 · Choose HOW the action happens
⭐SIMPLE
Habit, fact or action
I play.
🏃CONTINUOUS
Action in progress
I am playing.
✅PERFECT
Completed by a reference time
I have played.
⏳PERFECT CONTINUOUS
Duration up to a reference time
I have been playing.
STEP 3 · Put the time + pattern together

⏪ PAST

⭐ SimpleI played.
🏃 ContinuousI was playing.
✅ PerfectI had played.
⏳ Perfect ContinuousI had been playing.

⏺️ PRESENT

⭐ SimpleI play.
🏃 ContinuousI am playing.
✅ PerfectI have played.
⏳ Perfect ContinuousI have been playing.

⏩ FUTURE

⭐ SimpleI will play.
🏃 ContinuousI will be playing.
✅ PerfectI will have played.
⏳ Perfect ContinuousI will have been playing.
